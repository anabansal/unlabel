    What is Plainshot?

    Plainshot is a free photo metadata remover that runs in your browser. It strips the hidden information stored inside photo files (GPS location, camera and phone model, capture date, AI source tags and edit history) so you can post to Instagram, or anywhere else, without sharing personal details by accident.

    Every photo from a phone carries a hidden record next to the picture. On a typical phone photo it includes the exact coordinates where you took it, the phone model and sometimes its serial number, and the second it was taken. Editing apps add their own history, and AI tools add tags that mark the picture as AI-generated or AI-edited. None of it is visible in the photo, but anyone who gets the original file can read it.

    What Plainshot removes

    Location
    GPS coordinates, plus city and country fields, that pinpoint where a photo was taken.
    AI & source tags
    C2PA Content Credentials, IPTC DigitalSourceType (“trained algorithmic media”), and AI prompts saved inside PNG files.
    Camera & phone
    Make, model, lens, serial number and maker notes in the EXIF data.
    Date & time
    When the photo was taken, created and last changed.
    Edit history
    Editing software, Photoshop and Lightroom history, and gallery-editor undo data.
    Hidden preview image
    Small thumbnails inside the file that can still show the photo as it was before cropping.
    Name & copyright
    Author, owner and copyright fields, captions, keywords and comments.
    Extra embedded data
    Depth maps, HDR gain maps, motion-photo data and anything tucked after the end of the image.

    What stays exactly the same

    Your photo looks identical. Plainshot does not re-compress anything.

    • Every pixel. For JPEG, PNG and WebP, the compressed picture data is copied byte for byte, so there is zero quality loss.
    • Colors. The color profile (for example Display P3 on iPhone photos) is kept, so colors don’t shift or wash out.
    • Orientation. The rotation flag is kept on its own, so a portrait photo never turns sideways.

    How to remove hidden data from a photo

    1. Add your photos. Drag them onto this page, paste them, or tap “choose photos”. One photo or a hundred.
    2. Check what came off. Each photo gets a card listing what was removed, such as location, camera details and AI tags.
    3. Download and post. Save each photo, or use “Download all” for a ZIP, then upload the clean copies to Instagram yourself.

    Why clean photos before posting to Instagram?

    When you upload, Instagram receives your original file with everything in it. Meta has said it reads industry-standard AI signals in that file, namely C2PA Content Credentials and IPTC metadata, to decide whether to add an “AI info” label. A small edit made with an AI tool, like removing a stray object, can be enough to trigger it on a real photo.

    Location is the bigger worry. The file you post is often the same one you send in chats, email or other sites that keep the original. Cleaning it first means your home or school coordinates never leave your phone in the first place.

    Questions

    Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

    No. Plainshot runs entirely in your browser. Your photos are read and cleaned on your own device and are never sent to a server. You can turn off Wi-Fi after the page loads and it still works.

    Does removing metadata reduce photo quality?

    No. For JPEG, PNG and WebP files, Plainshot removes only the hidden data blocks and copies the picture data byte for byte. Nothing is re-compressed, so quality, colors and orientation stay exactly the same.

    Will this remove the “AI info” label on Instagram?

    It removes the AI tags stored in the file, including C2PA Content Credentials, IPTC DigitalSourceType and AI prompt text, which are the signals Meta has said it reads. It can’t remove invisible watermarks woven into the pixels themselves (such as Google SynthID), and Instagram may still add a label based on other signals or your own disclosure.

    Does Instagram show my photo’s location?

    Instagram doesn’t show the GPS data from your file to other people, but it does receive the full original when you upload. Removing location first means the coordinates never leave your phone, which also protects you when you share the same file elsewhere.

    Which file types work?

    JPEG, PNG and WebP are cleaned with no quality change at all. HEIC, AVIF and other formats your browser can open are saved as a new high-quality JPEG with no metadata. On iPhone, Safari hands over JPEG copies automatically.

    Does it work on iPhone and Android?

    Yes. Open this page in Safari, Chrome or any modern browser, tap “choose photos”, and pick from your library. Downloads go to your Files or Downloads folder, ready to post.

    Is Plainshot free?

    Yes. No sign-up, no ads, no limits. The code is public on GitHub under the MIT license, so anyone can check that photos stay on the device.
